## 13. Booking and Cancellation Rules

- Deposits are nonrefundable for no-shows.
- Customers receive a 15-minute lateness window.
- Sessions receive only a five-minute end-of-session grace period.
- Any session extension must:
  1. Be communicated to the engineer
  2. Be approved
  3. Be confirmed in-house
- Minimum booking age: 18
- The client is financially responsible for damage caused during the booking.
- Prohibited conduct:
  - Smoking
  - Guns or weapons
  - Illegal activity

**POLICY REQUIREMENT:** The published no-smoking rule must match the rule enforced inside the studio. Do not advertise a restriction that is knowingly ignored.

## 14. Programs and Pages to Keep

### Keep and Emphasize
- Creators Club loyalty and rewards program
- Community playlist
- Booking pages
- Service pages
- Inquiry/contact path

The Creators Club is considered essential because it rewards repeat customers and differentiates Clickbait from studios that provide no loyalty benefit.

### Simplify or Remove
- Remove the standalone Listen page.
- Place the community playlist inside a stronger Portfolio, Work, or Community section.
- Remove the Event Special page.
- Do not expose empty or unnecessary customer-account pages publicly.
- Simplify the loyalty program so customers can understand how rewards are earned and redeemed.
