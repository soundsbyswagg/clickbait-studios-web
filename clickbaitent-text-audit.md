---

## Executive Summary of Text Quality
The site surfaces a lot of repetitive, vague, and filler text. Strong on contact details and basic service list, weak on specificity, consistency, and professionalism in copy. Many pages repeat the exact same nav/footer blocks and hero images.

### Good
- Consistent address, phone (470-621-1417), email (support@clickbaitent.com) across footers.
- Clear pricing "from" values and durations on book/services pages.
- Detailed cancellation policies on individual service pages (good for trust).
- Team member names + titles on About.
- 24-hour / everyday claim is direct.
- Loyalty program has explicit earn/redeem rules (even if complex).
- "Record with CLICKBAIT to be featured!" call to action.
- Service categories on /services page give high-level overview (Recording, Mixing & Mastering, etc.).

### Bad / Insufficient / Misleading / Unclear
- "A Session W/ Engineer" vs "B Session W/ Engineer" — no distinction ever explained.
- Prices are always "From $X" with no hourly rate, inclusions, or what "finish records" actually delivers.
- "CHEAPEST RATES IN THE CITY" — unsubstantiated claim, no evidence.
- "MORE THAN RAP!" / "CLICKBAITS BUILD A RAPPER PROGRAM" — vague title + bullet list only; no schedule or deliverables.
- Email mismatch: footers say support@clickbaitent.com; service policies list squadgoalsentllc@gmail.com.
- Social icons on About page point to Wix defaults, not real accounts.
- "Over 50 sessions recorded per month" — unverified boast.
- "helps you finish records... writing, recording, production, mixing, mastering, and publishing guidance" — ambitious claim not backed by detailed offerings.
- Listen page is almost all filler.
- Duplicate pages (Event Special is copy of home).
- "Loading days..." placeholder still visible on booking pages.
- "Use tab to navigate through the menu items." appears as visible text.
- Branding inconsistency: Clickbait Studios / Clickbait Ent / Clickbait Enterprises.

### What Needs to Be Culled (or heavily rewritten)
- All instances of "ARE YOU HEARING THIS???" (repeated 14+ times on Listen).
- Duplicate paragraphs on About ("Clickbait Ent a marketing media and audio specialists entity...").
- Entire Event Special page (near-identical copy of home).
- Repetitive nav/footer blocks in every extraction (they dominate the text).
- Filler like "Pull Up!", "Like a DISCOUNT?", "Plan Your Visit".
- Overly long loyalty reward lists if they stay this complex.
- Placeholder text ("Loading days...").
- "Use tab to navigate..." menu instructions.
- "CHEAPEST RATES IN THE CITY" banners and similar hype without proof.
- Repeated hero images and "RECORD. CREATE. AMPLIFY." on every page.
- Vague service names and "Explore Plans" that go nowhere.

## Recommendations for 10/10 Text
1. Cull all repetition (filler lines, duplicate pages, repeated nav text in extraction).
2. Replace vague service names with clear ones (e.g. "Studio A - Engineer Included (2-8hr)").
3. Make every price specific (e.g. "$60/hr with engineer, min 2hr").
4. Remove or substantiate all claims ("cheapest", "50 sessions", "finish records").
5. Fix social links and email addresses to be consistent.
6. Delete or merge duplicate pages (Event Special).
7. Rewrite Listen page to 2-3 sentences max + real playlist embed.
8. Simplify loyalty to top 5 rewards.
9. Add real portfolio / testimonials / before-after text.
10. Keep the strong cancellation policy and contact footer.

End of audit.
